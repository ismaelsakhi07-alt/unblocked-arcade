Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

PixelBreak Arcade is a static, free browser-game website. This policy describes the current site implementation. It must be updated before introducing new advertising, analytics, accounts, or other third-party services.

Information handled by the games

We do not provide user accounts or ask for names, email addresses, or other personal information. The site saves game best scores in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device and can be removed by clearing site data in your browser.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses and request information, to deliver the website and protect its infrastructure. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Advertising and analytics

The current website has an advertising placeholder but does not load an advertising network, behavioral advertising code, or analytics scripts. If these are added, this policy will be updated before activation, including disclosures and consent controls as required by applicable law.

Children

Our games do not require accounts or direct submission of personal information. The site is not currently configured to run targeted advertising. If we later operate a service directed to children under 13, we will evaluate and implement applicable child privacy requirements, including COPPA, before introducing relevant data collection or advertising.

Contact

A dedicated privacy contact address has not yet been configured. Site owners should publish a working contact method before applying for advertising programs or collecting user data.
